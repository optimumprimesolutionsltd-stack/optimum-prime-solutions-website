## TIPS FOR SUCCESS

1. Personalize each email - use their name and reference something specific
2. Keep emails short - 150-200 words maximum
3. Make it easy - provide exact anchor text and URL
4. Offer value - referral discount, guest post, or co-marketing
5. Follow up once - polite reminder after 7-10 days
6. Use phone when possible - calls get higher response than email alone
7. Track everything - know which channels work best
